Quick start / 01

Three steps to the finish.

  • Customize: choose your paint and wheels. Your ride is ready from the start.
  • Build a track: add straightaways, corners, chicanes, and jumps. Four or more sections make a circuit.
  • Race: use W or ↑ to accelerate, A/D or arrows to steer, Space for a short boost, and S/↓ to brake. Stay on the road!
  • Beat the AI to earn cash. Spend it on wheel sets; your balance and story progress save automatically.
