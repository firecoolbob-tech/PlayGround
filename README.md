## Nightshift Garage

A self-contained browser racing game. Open `index.html` directly, or serve this folder locally:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 3002
```

Then visit [http://localhost:3002](http://localhost:3002).

### How to play

- **Customize:** choose your paint color and equip wheel sets. Rally and Aero wheels can be purchased with race winnings.
- **Track builder:** add straights, corners, chicanes, and jumps. Use at least four sections to make a raceable circuit.
- **Race:** hold **W** or **↑** to accelerate, use **A/D** or **←/→** to steer, hold **Space** for a short boost, and use **S/↓** to brake. Stay on the road to keep your speed up. On touch screens, hold and drag on the race view to accelerate and steer.
- **Story mode:** finish in the top three to earn chapter bonuses and unlock the next challenge.

Cash, car setup, track layout, and story progress save in your browser automatically. Sound effects can be enabled from the header.
